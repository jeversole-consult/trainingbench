# TB-SPRINT-NNN: <short sprint name>

**Status**: Planned | Active | Closed
**Started**: YYYY-MM-DD
**Closed**: YYYY-MM-DD (or `—` while open)
**Goal**: One sentence. What does this sprint exist to accomplish?

## Scope

The stories committed to this sprint. A story is "in scope" only if
its file exists and is at least Proposed. Don't list ideas here —
list stories.

| ID | Title | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [TB-US-XXX](../user-stories/tb-us-XXX.md) | ... | Proposed \| In Progress \| Completed |

## Out of scope

What this sprint deliberately is **not** taking on. Use this section
to push back against scope creep. Reference story IDs where
applicable so the reader can see where deferred work is tracked.

- Story IDs queued for a later sprint
- Categories of work the sprint explicitly avoids

## Notes

Free-form context. Why this sprint, why now, what changed mid-sprint,
what landed alongside the stories that was platform groundwork rather
than tracked story work. Keep this honest — the sprint file is the
record of what actually happened, not the plan you wish had happened.

## Review / Retrospective

Filled in at sprint close. Three short sections:

- **What worked** — methodology, scoping, or technical decisions worth
  carrying into the next sprint.
- **What didn't** — friction, miscalls, or assumptions that didn't
  hold up. Be specific.
- **Carry-over** — stories or sub-tasks not finished in this sprint
  and where they go next (next sprint, queued, dropped).
