# Formula sheet — grade-school math reference

Compact reference for arithmetic, basic algebra, and the geometry
facts that appear in grade-school math word problems.

## Arithmetic operations

| Operation | Form | Note |
|---|---|---|
| Addition | `a + b` | Order doesn't matter |
| Subtraction | `a - b` | Order matters |
| Multiplication | `a × b` or `a · b` | Order doesn't matter |
| Division | `a ÷ b` or `a / b` | Order matters |

**Order of operations (PEMDAS):**
Parentheses → Exponents → Multiplication/Division (left to right) → Addition/Subtraction (left to right)

## Fractions

| Operation | Formula |
|---|---|
| Addition (same denominator) | `a/c + b/c = (a + b)/c` |
| Addition (different denom.) | `a/b + c/d = (ad + bc)/(bd)` |
| Multiplication | `(a/b) × (c/d) = (ac)/(bd)` |
| Division | `(a/b) ÷ (c/d) = (ad)/(bc)` |

**Simplify** a fraction by dividing numerator and denominator by
the same number: `6/8 = 3/4` (both divided by 2).

## Percent

- **Percent to decimal:** divide by 100. `35% = 0.35`
- **Decimal to percent:** multiply by 100. `0.42 = 42%`
- **Percent of a number:** `P% of N = (P/100) × N`
  - Example: 25% of 80 = `(25/100) × 80 = 20`
- **Finding the percent:** `(part / whole) × 100`
  - Example: 12 is what % of 60? `(12/60) × 100 = 20%`

## Ratio

- A ratio `a : b` expresses that for every `a` of one thing, there
  are `b` of another.
- **Equivalent ratios:** multiply both parts by the same number.
  `3 : 4 = 6 : 8 = 9 : 12`
- **Finding an unknown part:** if the ratio is `a : b` and the
  total is `T`, then the first quantity is `T × a / (a + b)`.
  - Example: 20 marbles in ratio 2:3 → `20 × 2/5 = 8` and
    `20 × 3/5 = 12`.

## Rate and distance

| Quantity | Formula |
|---|---|
| Distance | `d = r × t` (rate × time) |
| Rate | `r = d / t` |
| Time | `t = d / r` |

**Unit discipline:** rate and time must share the same time unit.
If rate is in miles/hour and time is in minutes, convert minutes
to hours first (`minutes / 60`).

## Perimeter and area

| Shape | Perimeter | Area |
|---|---|---|
| Square | `4 × side` | `side²` |
| Rectangle | `2 × (length + width)` | `length × width` |
| Triangle | sum of 3 sides | `(1/2) × base × height` |
| Circle | `2 × π × radius` (circumference) | `π × radius²` |

## Volume (common solids)

| Shape | Volume |
|---|---|
| Cube | `side³` |
| Rectangular prism | `length × width × height` |
| Cylinder | `π × radius² × height` |

## Simple algebra

- **Equation:** two expressions set equal: `x + 3 = 10`
- **Solving for `x`:** apply the same operation to both sides to
  isolate the variable.
  - `x + 3 = 10` → subtract 3 from both sides → `x = 7`
  - `2x = 14` → divide both sides by 2 → `x = 7`
  - `x/3 = 5` → multiply both sides by 3 → `x = 15`
