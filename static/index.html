📚 Training User Guide

Objective

Training helps improve the Agent. Feedback on the Agent's answers creates high-quality training data that will make the Agent smarter and more accurate over time.

How It Works

  1. Ask Questions — Type questions relevant to your loaded knowledge base.
  2. Review the Answer — Read the Agent's response carefully.
  3. Rate the Response — Choose one:
    • ✓ Acceptable — The answer is correct, complete, and helpful.
    • ✗ Needs Improvement — The answer is wrong, incomplete, or unclear.
  4. Provide Corrections (for "Needs Improvement") — When you mark an answer as needing improvement, you'll be asked to provide the correct answer or explain what's wrong.

Tips for Effective Training

  • Ask a variety of questions — easy, medium, and difficult
  • Be honest and accurate in your ratings
  • When providing corrections, be specific and complete
  • Don't skip the feedback — it's what makes the training data valuable!

What Happens Next?

Your rated Q&A pairs are stored in a training database. Administrators will use this data to:

  • Create training sets from your feedback
  • Fine-tune the Agent with correct answers
  • Improve the system's accuracy for everyone
Questions? Contact your system administrator if you need help or have questions about the training process.

Component Details

RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)

Your documents are chunked and embedded as vectors in a ChromaDB database. When you ask a question, the system performs semantic search to find the most relevant chunks, which provide context to the language model.

Spreadsheet Calculator

When questions involve numerical data, the calculator component automatically extracts values, performs computations, and integrates results into the final answer. This enables financial analysis, data comparisons, and mathematical operations.

Language Model

The system uses either standard Ollama models (like Llama 3.1 8B) or fine-tuned PEFT adapters trained on domain-specific data. PEFT adapters provide specialized knowledge while maintaining the base model's general capabilities.
